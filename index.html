Privacy Policy

Kitchen Countdown (also called PantryTracker) · Effective October 4, 2026

Kitchen Countdown helps you and the people you live with keep track of the food in your kitchen and what to use before it expires. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app doesn't track you across apps or websites, and your data is never sold.
  • Photos, receipts and voice are processed on your iPhone. The images and audio themselves aren't uploaded.
  • Your kitchen syncs through our server so everyone in your household sees the same list.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Settings → Delete account.

What we collect

DataWhy
An account identifier. If you use Sign in with Apple, the email Apple shares with us, which is often a private relay address that hides your real one. To keep your kitchen when you reinstall or switch phones.
The display name you enter Shown to other members of your kitchen.
Kitchen (household) names, members and invite codes To share one inventory with the people you invite.
Items you add: name, category, quantity, place (fridge, freezer, pantry), dates, and whether you used or tossed them This is the inventory itself, plus the history shown in Insights.
Barcodes and the product names you confirm Kept in a shared product list so the next scan of the same barcode is instant. This list contains products, not who scanned them.

Everyone in a kitchen can see that kitchen's items and members' display names. People who aren't members can't.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Camera and photos. Barcodes, receipts, and photos you share into the app from Photos are read on your device using Apple's built-in recognition. The app only sees photos you choose, and doesn't upload them.
  • Voice. When you add items by voice, speech is turned into text on your iPhone when the device supports it. On older devices Apple's speech service may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Reminders and the widget. Expiry reminders are scheduled on your phone, and the Home Screen widget reads a copy of your upcoming items stored on your phone.
  • Settings such as theme and reminder time.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in that sync your kitchen.
  • Open Food Facts, a public food database, receives a barcode when you scan a product it might know. Only the barcode is sent, nothing about you.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and on-device text and speech recognition.
  • Expo delivers app updates. Like any download, these requests include technical details such as your IP address and app version.

These providers handle data only to provide their service to the app.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while you use the app. Items you mark used or tossed stay in your history so Insights can show trends. When you delete your account, your account and name are deleted right away. Kitchens where you were the only member are deleted with all their items. Kitchens you share stay with the other members, and items you added there remain part of their inventory. Server backups may retain deleted data for a short period before it's overwritten.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account.
  • Leave or delete a kitchen: Settings → Leave kitchen.
  • Edit or remove items at any time from the item's details.
  • Camera, photos, microphone and notifications are optional and can be turned off in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

Kitchen Countdown isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
